## Fallback 1 — SportsDataAPI (free tier / $19.99 route)

**Enquiry to send (draft, founder sends; every item needs a written answer):**

Subject: Historical tennis data — personal model-training use, pre-purchase questions

1. Which **historical years** of tennis results are available, and at which price tier
   (free vs $19.99)? Is the archive complete for those years or rolling-window only?
2. **ATP / WTA / Challenger coverage** — which tours and levels, singles completeness?
3. Could you share a **sample payload** for (a) a completed match result and (b) a
   ranking snapshot, so I can verify fields (winner, score, retirement flag, dates)?
4. Do your terms permit **training a private model used for my own profit-seeking
   betting** (nothing resold or republished)? Which clause covers this?
5. May I **retain raw downloaded data** after cancellation? May I **retain models
   derived** from it?
6. Is processing in a **private cloud workspace** permitted?
7. Is **personal betting use** of model outputs permitted?
8. What is the **minimum cost to backfill 2021-01 → 2026-05** (one-off preferred), and
   the **minimum commitment** / cancellation terms?
