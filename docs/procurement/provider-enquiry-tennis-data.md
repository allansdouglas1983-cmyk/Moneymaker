**Subject:** Permission request — personal use of Tennis-Data historical results files

Dear Tennis-Data,

I am an individual (not a company) building a private, personal tennis match-probability
model, and I would like to use your historical ATP/WTA results files as an input. Before
using them I want to make sure I have your explicit permission for exactly what I would
be doing. Could you confirm in writing whether the following are permitted:

1. **Bulk download** of the historical season files (ATP and WTA) from your site;
2. **Model training for private, profit-seeking use** — the model informs my own
   personal betting on Betfair; nothing is sold, published, or offered to anyone else;
3. **Retention of the raw downloaded files** on my own storage for reproducibility;
4. **Processing in a private cloud workspace** (a private, access-controlled development
   environment I use instead of a local machine — not public hosting);
5. **Retention of models derived from the data** after any future change to my use of
   the site;
6. **Personal Betfair use** of the model's outputs (my own account only);
7. I would **not redistribute** your files or any recognisable extract of them in any
   form — please confirm this matches your expectations;
8. Whether you require any **attribution** (and in what form) for private, non-published
   use.

If any of these requires a licence, a fee, or is simply not permitted, I would be
grateful to know. I am happy to comply with whatever conditions you set, and I will not
use the files until I have your written answer.

Thank you for maintaining the resource.

Kind regards,
[Founder name]
