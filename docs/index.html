BET_CANDIDATE is structurally unreachable. No branch in the scoring code can produce it. The status you will see on a candidate-shaped price is BET_CANDIDATE_DISABLED.

Break-even is 1/(1+(O−1)(1−c)) at 2% commission, not 1/O. Commission is charged on net winnings, so it raises the bar twice.

Every edge is shown as a range, not a point. The low end deducts the execution-cost band: half the frozen Roll spread for the market's liquidity stratum, and — because a manually entered price carries no age — the pooled conservative spread (p90, 7.8% of the price), never a claimed stratum. A quoted point edge without that band would be pretending fills are free; TE-0020 showed they are not.

The model beats the closing price by +0.001064 nats over 63,676 out-of-sample matches (95% CI +0.000639 to +0.001476). That is a forecasting result, not a money result.
