## Context from the STAGE3-0002 audit (outcome-blind)

Of 1252 usable identifying derivatives, only **56** showed `OBSERVABLY_NON_REDUNDANT_UPDATES`
and **1196** were `INDEPENDENCE_UNRESOLVED`. The historical feed does not expose internal
order origin, so non-redundancy cannot be proven from it. This record seeks an external,
scientifically defensible basis before any independence is assumed.

## Questions (to be answered by external research, with sources + dates)

1. **Cross-matching mechanics.** How does Betfair's cross-matching between Match Odds and
   its Asian derivative markets (Total Games, Game Handicap) actually work, and what (if
   anything) is publicly / officially documented about internal order origin?
2. **Observable independence.** Given only a historical price/timestamp feed, what is the
   defensible standard for concluding a derivative price carries information NOT already in
   Match Odds — beyond mere timestamp differences (which this audit already rejects as
   insufficient)?
3. **Redundancy tests.** What established methods (from market-microstructure literature or
   practitioner evidence) distinguish an independently-quoted market from a deterministic
   re-expression of another market's price?
4. **Applicability to tennis derivatives.** Whether such methods can be applied to the June
   ADVANCED corpus without new data purchase, and what their power/limitations would be at
   the observed coverage (494 usable at 300 s; STRICT-concentrated).
5. **Minimum standard for activation.** The minimum defensible evidence that would justify
   treating Total Games / Game Handicap as an independent constraint in a latent model.
6. **Source + date.** Official / literature sources, with dates.
