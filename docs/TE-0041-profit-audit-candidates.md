## 1. Capture each venue's own quote timestamp (`last_update`) — without it the line-shopping dataset cannot answer the question it was built for

**Status:** OPEN · **Area:** Data capture / supabase/functions/tips (board.ts, index.ts) + schema · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** hours

**Mechanism.** Line shopping selects the MAXIMUM price across venues. When venues update asynchronously, the max is systematically the venue whose quote has drifted least toward new information — i.e. the stalest one. That is the classic winner's-curse of line shopping: you win the comparison precisely when the counterparty has not repriced, which is adverse selection, not edge. With only `captured_at` stored, a fresh Smarkets quote and one frozen four hours ago are indistinguishable in the record, so the shopping gain and the staleness artefact cannot be separated — even retrospectively, because the timestamps were never kept. Every refresh that runs without this writes rows that will have to be discarded or heavily caveated. It is a strictly-additive capture fix that unblocks candidate 5.

**Expected magnitude.** No direct ROI. It is the precondition for measuring a quantity plausibly worth 0.0%-0.3% ROI, and the guard against booking a false positive of similar size. Concretely: TE-0027 already measured that a 5-8 minute price delay is worth 0.18-0.21% ROI on the SAME venue; cross-venue staleness at the board's 6-hour refresh cadence is a strictly larger effect, so an uncontrolled max-across-venues estimator could easily fabricate a 'gain' of several tenths of a percent that is pure staleness. Data-loss cost of not doing it: at 3 refreshes/day x ~20 venues x ~30 matches, roughly 1,800 unusable observation-rows per day.

**Evidence.** board.ts:117-150 (`VenueQuote` has no timestamp field); index.ts:467-484 (insert writes a single `captured_at` for the whole batch); tables.sql:86-90 (no per-venue time column); supabase/schema/objects.md:41 ('Feeds line-shopping and closing-line-value analysis'); TE-0027 (delay on one venue costs 0.18-0.21% ROI); TE-0037 §'Done today' explicitly names venue capture as raw material for line shopping, and §4 says 'give it a month' — a month of timestamp-less data.

**How to verify.** After one refresh, `select venue, count(*), min(captured_at - venue_last_update), median(...), max(...) from tennis.price_observations group by venue` — a healthy exchange should show small, non-degenerate ages; if every venue shows an identical age the field is being populated from the wrong source. Regression: the board.test.ts fixture with per-venue `last_update` must fail on the current implementation and pass after.

**Risks.** The Odds API's `last_update` semantics may be 'when our crawler last saw this bookmaker' rather than 'when the venue last changed this price' — those are different quantities and the weaker one only bounds staleness from below. Record the field verbatim and label it as a provider timestamp (SPEC-020 knowledge-time vocabulary already distinguishes provider timestamp from ingestion receive time); do not rename it into a claim it cannot support. Minor schema/migration coordination since the live DB is ahead of the committed schema in places (the `venue_dispersion` view exists in objects.md with no SQL in the repo).

**Rule conflicts.** NONE. Pure observational capture; no order, no in-play (the existing `captured_at >= commence_time` exclusion in clv() must be applied to the new field too), no model or threshold change.

## 2. Record the decision venue and its commission on every prediction, and restrict the CLV monitor to one venue — venue mixing is contaminating the repo's single most important live measurement

**Status:** **CLOSED/MEASURED** · **Area:** Evidence layer / predictions schema + /clv endpoint · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** days

**Mechanism.** The residual model v3 is anchored to the EXCHANGE de-vigged price (TE-0013/TE-0019 re-anchoring). A bookmaker's overround is not distributed symmetrically — it is loaded disproportionately on the outsider — so proportional de-vigging of a Pinnacle quote and of a Betfair quote produce systematically different probabilities for the same true market view. Worked example at true p=0.70: a 3% bookmaker overround with favourite-longshot loading de-vigs to ~0.689, a 1.1% exchange overround to ~0.698 — a ~0.9 probability-point venue offset. CLV as built measures drift in de-vigged probability points on the picked side; TE-0027's measured drift is ~+0.0026 in logits, i.e. roughly 0.05 probability points at p=0.5. A venue offset of ~0.9 points is roughly 18x the signal. Even a 10% fallback rate injects ~0.09 points of bias — still larger than the effect being measured. Separately, a prediction minted from a Pinnacle price and one from a Betfair price are currently pooled in the same ledger denominator with no way to separate them.

**Expected magnitude.** Removes a bias of order 0.1-0.9 de-vigged probability points from a signal of order 0.05 points, scaling with the (currently unmeasured, unpersisted) fallback rate. Under the CLAUDE.md standing rule that a surprisingly good backtest is a suspected bug, this is the mechanism by which the CLV monitor could read strongly positive for a reason that has nothing to do with the model. TE-0037 ranks 'let CLV accumulate, then read it before anything else changes' as the #2 action and the earliest honest signal of a live edge; this is a correctness precondition on that read, not an enhancement.

**Evidence.** tables.sql:73-85 (predictions has `commission` but no venue column); index.ts:453-458 and 489-500 (venue chosen by fallback, recorded only on the fixture); index.ts:501-505 (fixtures merge-duplicates destroys mint-time source); index.ts:903-906 (clv hard-filters observations to betfair_ex_uk while decision price is venue-agnostic); TE-0003 Finding 2 and the MIDPOINT vs BEST_BACK log-loss gap of 0.0032 nats — nearly 3x the model's whole +0.0011 nat edge — showing that price-definition changes of this size dominate the effect being measured; scoring.ts:31 (COMMISSION is a single constant applied 'even to a bookmaker quote').

**How to verify.** (a) Persist and report the fallback rate: `select price_venue, count(*) from tennis.predictions group by 1` — if betfair_ex_uk is ~100% the contamination is nil and the fix is cheap insurance; if not, the current /clv figure is quantifiably wrong. (b) Re-run /clv on the same window before and after the venue restriction and report both; a material move IS the measured contamination. (c) Unit test: a prediction row minted at pinnacle plus a betfair closing observation must be counted as an exclusion, not scored.

**Risks.** `predictions` is append-only and check-constrained; adding a NOT NULL column needs a default for historical rows — use an explicit `UNKNOWN` sentinel, not a backfilled guess, or the record acquires a fabricated provenance (SPEC-036: a missing field is explicit, NEVER silently substituted). Restricting CLV to same-venue pairs will shrink the denominator; that is correct and the excluded count must be reported (the repo's standing 'universe in equals filled plus excluded' discipline), not hidden. Do not retro-grade any existing CLV number as if it had been venue-clean.

**Rule conflicts.** NONE. This narrows what may be pooled and adds provenance. It does not tune a threshold, alter the model, or change what is bet. It strengthens SPEC-051 (the three prices are distinct) and SPEC-036 (a missing probability is explicit) rather than relaxing them.

## 3. Refuse a decision price from a venue no UK resident can transact at — the TypeScript board can mint an edge from Pinnacle, the exact error TE-0013 corrected in Python and guarded with a test

**Status:** **CLOSED/MEASURED** · **Area:** supabase/functions/tips/board.ts (BOOK_PREFERENCE) — governance/consistency defect · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** hours

**Mechanism.** An edge, break-even and BET_CANDIDATE_DISABLED status computed from a price that cannot be taken is a number about a counterfactual. TE-0013 documents that this project already published a money table led by Pinnacle and by the panel maximum, and that the correction required a registry plus tests because 'nothing in the data says so'. The same failure has been re-created on the serving side, where it additionally feeds the append-only prediction ledger. A secondary consequence: bookmaker venues restrict consistent winners (venues.py:20-23), so even a UK-reachable bookmaker price has an expiry date it does not control — which is why the decision set should be exchanges only, and why TE-0037 already rejected bookmaker line shopping on exactly this ground.

**Expected magnitude.** Not a profit gain — a defect closure. Its size equals the fallback rate to Pinnacle (currently unmeasured because `books_used` is returned per refresh and never persisted). Every such prediction is an unusable ledger row: it enters the CLV denominator, the scorecard and the ROI record with a price nobody here could have taken. Prevents the recurrence of a documented published-error class at a cost of about twenty lines and one test.

**Evidence.** board.ts:17-22 vs tennis_edge/venues.py:130-139 (`Access.CLOSED_TO_UK`, 'its return is not money'); tests/unit/tennis_edge/test_venues.py `test_nothing_in_the_benchmark_list_is_reachable_from_the_uk`; TE-0013 §'What is guarded now' ('A future table cannot be led by Pinnacle without deleting a test' — true in Python, false in TypeScript); TE-0037 rejection row 'Pinnacle as an additional benchmark | Closed to UK customers, so never a return'.

**How to verify.** `select source, count(*) from tennis.fixtures group by 1` and the persisted `books_used` counter (add it to `config` or a small log table) give the current Pinnacle/Smarkets/Matchbook fallback rate — that number IS the size of the defect. Then: the new board.test.ts case must fail against the current `BOOK_PREFERENCE` and pass after; `make verify` and the golden.json board vectors must stay green.

**Risks.** Removing Pinnacle from the decision ladder slightly reduces board coverage on matches Betfair does not quote, which reduces the number of predictions minted — i.e. less evidence collected, the cost TE-0037 flags under 'more bets at the same edge'. That is the right trade: a prediction at an untakeable price is not evidence. Do not compensate by adding UK bookmakers to the decision set; that reintroduces the restriction problem. Keep Pinnacle in `allVenueQuotes` capture — deleting it would destroy a genuine sharpness benchmark (TE-0013 measured the model at +1.48% vs a −2.08% control against Pinnacle).

**Rule conflicts.** NONE. It narrows what may price a bet. It touches no threshold, no model coefficient and no stake.

## 4. A founder-verified per-venue commission registry with an exact-arithmetic after-commission comparator — and the finding that commission is a tie-break, never a price override

**Status:** OPEN · **Area:** Pricing / scoring.ts + governance registry · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** days

**Mechanism.** After-commission net winnings per unit stake are `(O-1)*(1-c)`, so the venue ranking is by that product, not by O. The audit question asks when a nominally worse price is actually better; the exact answer, computed over the Betfair ladder, is: a 0.5 percentage-point commission advantage is worth only 0.09 to 0.38 of ONE Betfair tick across the whole odds range (0.26 ticks at 2.0, 0.20 at 3.0, 0.13 at 6.0). A rival at 2.00 on 1.5% commission needs Betfair to beat 2.00510 to win — inside a single 0.02 tick. So commission NEVER overturns a venue that is even one tick better; it decides only EXACT price ties, which on exchanges quoting the same liquid market are common. Correctly framed, this is a free tie-break rule with no price risk, not a reason to take a worse number.

**Expected magnitude.** On an exact tie, routing to a venue 0.5pp cheaper is worth p*(O-1)*0.005 per unit stake: +0.115% of stake at odds 1.3, +0.250% at 2.0, +0.333% at 3.0, +0.450% at 10.0 — roughly +0.2% to +0.3% ROI averaged over the bet book's odds distribution, times the share of bets that are exact ties. Compare: TE-0027 measured 5-minute price delay at 0.18-0.21% ROI and the repo treats that as material enough to act on. On the frozen +2.33% headline it is about a tenth of the point estimate, and it is one of the very few improvements available that carries no adverse-selection risk. UNKNOWN until the tie rate is measured (candidate 5). Commission facts themselves are UNKNOWN until founder-verified — the 1.5%/2% figures above are illustrative arithmetic, not asserted rates.

**Evidence.** scoring.ts:31 and :454 (single global commission); index.ts:703 (hardcoded 0.02 on real bets); DR-TENNIS-MICROSTRUCTURE-001 assessment §'Commission — resolved by founder statement': 2% is a labelled Basic-package modelling assumption, with the Australasian-events MBR caveat still live; TE-0003 Finding 2 ('commission ... adding ~1% of turnover at even money'); SPEC-110 (exact arithmetic, floats refused); SPEC-093 (stale facts fail).

**How to verify.** Property tests mirroring SPEC-110's declared metamorphic properties: increasing commission never improves any EV; a float-typed rate is refused; identity (equal rates) reproduces the current comparator exactly; the golden.json board vectors are unchanged when every venue carries 0.02. Governance check: a test asserting that a venue lacking `verified_by_founder_at`, or past `recheck_by`, produces no status at all.

**Risks.** Highest-risk item on governance grounds and must be built defensively. (a) An LLM must never set or infer a rate — the registry is founder-verified data, and an unverified venue must produce a typed refusal, not a default. (b) Lowering the commission for a venue lowers its break-even and therefore turns some rows into BET_CANDIDATE_DISABLED: if the rate were ever chosen to make more rows qualify that is threshold-fitting through the back door. Freeze the registry with a version id, record it in the trial ledger, and require that a rate change be a separate human-approved commit citing the operator's published terms. (c) The Australasian-events Market Base Rate exception means Betfair's own rate is not even constant within Betfair; encode it as a labelled per-event condition or refuse those events, never silently average. (d) Betfair commission is on NET MARKET winnings (SPEC-080/054); a per-bet rate is only valid under one-selection-per-market, which v1 already enforces — the registry must assert that precondition rather than assume it.

**Rule conflicts.** NONE if built as specified. It would VIOLATE the rules if (i) the LLM populated a rate, (ii) rates were chosen against observed bet counts or returns, or (iii) it were shipped as an automatic routing change rather than a display/measurement change pending a governed decision. All three are excluded above.

## 5. Pre-registered retrospective read: what line shopping across exchanges would actually have been worth, with the staleness control declared in advance

**Status:** OPEN · **Area:** Evidence / analysis over tennis.price_observations · **Implementable now:** False · **Effort:** weeks

**Mechanism.** This is the only honest way to size the venue question, and the strata are what separate a real gain from the winner's curse. Structural prior, stated up front so the result is not a surprise either way: Betfair carries the overwhelming majority of exchange tennis liquidity, its book is typically about one tick wide pre-off (mildbyte via DR-TENNIS-MICROSTRUCTURE-001), and secondary exchanges are linked to it by arbitrageurs — so a secondary venue is usually equal or worse, and when it is better it is disproportionately because it has not repriced. Arithmetic of the upside: one tick gained at even money is 0.02 of odds on 0.98 net winnings, i.e. ~+0.98% of stake conditional on winning the comparison; so the gain is roughly (frequency of strict improvement) x (mean ticks gained) x ~1% ROI.

**Expected magnitude.** Prior band +0.0% to +0.2% ROI from raw price dispersion, plus the ~+0.2%-0.3% commission tie-break from candidate 4, MINUS an adverse-selection haircut that could plausibly take the raw-price component to zero or negative. Anchors: TE-0037 reports the first 8 captures had Betfair best every single time (0/8, n far too small to conclude anything but the right direction of the prior); TE-0003 measures the exchange best-back round-trip margin at a median 1.12%, so 0.56% per side is the entire theoretical pool a shopper is competing for and no shopping scheme can exceed it; TE-0013 measured 3.7 points of pure price selection between the panel MAXIMUM and panel AVERAGE — but that is 20 bookmakers at 4.4% margins, roughly four times the margin and seven times the venue count, so scaling it to 3 exchanges at 1.12% gives a low-tenths-of-a-percent expectation, consistent with the band above.

**Evidence.** index.ts:461-484 (all-venue capture already running); supabase/schema/objects.md:41 and :45 (`price_observations`, `venue_dispersion` view — 'Gross of commission by design', which is exactly the gap); TE-0037 item 4 ('First 8 captures: Betfair was the best exchange price every time, zero gain... Give it a month'); TE-0003 (exchange margin 1.12% round trip, median size GBP 250-253 on the liquid subset but GBP 57 across all singles); TE-0013 (panel-max vs panel-average = 3.7 points of pure price selection); TE-0027 (delay cost 0.18-0.21%).

**How to verify.** The pre-registration document (declared strata, estimator, falsification rule, window, alpha) committed BEFORE the read, per SPEC-094. Then the single read, reporting: exact-tie rate; strict-improvement rate and mean ticks gained; ROI delta with day-clustered CI; the three staleness strata separately; the count of observations excluded for cross-venue overround < 1.0.

**Risks.** THE DOMINANT CAVEAT, and it must appear in the finding's headline sentence: The Odds API publishes no SIZE for exchange quotes, so every price in `price_observations` is depth-blind. A secondary exchange showing a better number may have GBP 5 behind it. This makes the whole measurement an UPPER BOUND on the shopping gain, in exactly the way DR-TENNIS-MICROSTRUCTURE-001 says a quote-only claim must be labelled — the result is a hypothetical under a stated availability assumption, never a realised return. Partial mitigation with real force: under ADR 0020 the trial stake is at or near the exchange minimum, where thin depth is usually adequate — so the trial can test this cheaply, but the conclusion explicitly does NOT scale to larger stakes and must say so. Second risk: this analysis must not be re-run until it gives a pleasing answer. One declared window, one read, recorded in the ledger whichever way it falls, including FAIL_FUTILITY ('line shopping is a dead end here and we learned it for the price of a database table' — TE-0037's own framing).

**Rule conflicts.** NONE as specified, but two adjacent ideas are REJECTED here explicitly so they are never revisited. (1) CROSS-VENUE ARBITRAGE — backing player A at one exchange and player B at another when the combined book is under 1.0 — violates one-selection-per-market and the no-hedging prohibition outright. Sub-1.0 cross-venue books are used here ONLY as a staleness detector, never as a position. (2) Choosing a venue AFTER seeing which one produced the best backtest ROI is threshold-fitting and is forbidden; the venue rule must be declared before the read, and a positive result licenses only a governed change, not an immediate switch.

## 6. Record the venue and its actual commission on every real bet — the ADR 0020 manual trial is currently the only source of true fill evidence and it is venue-blind

**Status:** OPEN · **Area:** Real-money ledger / placed_bets + /bet endpoint · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** hours

**Mechanism.** ADR 0020's stated purpose is evidence — 'actual fills at actual prices, the one thing no historical archive can contain'. Secondary-exchange liquidity is precisely the quantity no free data source provides and no historical corpus contains; the only way to learn whether a Smarkets or Matchbook quote is takeable at trial stake is to take one and record what happened. Recording the venue makes the trial double as the line-shopping experiment at zero marginal cost and zero extra risk. Without it, a trial run partly at a second venue produces a pooled P&L from which neither the venue effect nor the fill evidence can be recovered, and TE-0020's cost band — the thing that makes the money verdict span zero — stays unmeasured against reality.

**Expected magnitude.** UNKNOWN in ROI terms and small in sample by construction (a bounded flat-stake trial). Its value is not return but the resolution of the binding unknown: TE-0020's Roll band half-width is ~1.25%, comparable to the entire +2.33% edge, and it is entirely an assumption about execution. Each real fill, with its quoted-versus-matched gap recorded, is a direct observation of that band. TE-0037 ranks the equivalent fill question as 'the single largest unknown in the money case and the reason the cost band spans zero'.

**Evidence.** tables.sql:49-55 (no venue column on placed_bets); index.ts:692-704 (commission hardcoded 0.02, no venue, no displayed size); ADR 0020 §'The trial protocol' (every bet recorded at placement with the actual matched price; account statement is truth for retirements); TE-0020 (band half-width ~1.25% vs a +2.33% point estimate); DR-TENNIS-MICROSTRUCTURE-001 (a realised-return claim requires real order-level execution records of your own — this is the only path to them).

**How to verify.** Refusal tests: recording a bet at an unregistered venue must return 409 and write no row; the budget guard must produce identical results whether bets are all at one venue or spread across three. Then, once real bets exist: `select venue, count(*), avg(quoted_odds_at_decision - matched_odds), sum(pnl) from tennis.placed_bets group by 1` — the middle column is the first direct measurement of realised execution cost this project will have had, and the one that can eventually narrow TE-0020's band from evidence instead of from a Roll proxy.

**Risks.** Governance, not technical. (a) Multiple venues must NOT become multiple loss budgets: `budgetState` already aggregates over all `placed_bets` rows regardless of venue and must keep doing so — a per-venue budget would let one venue's balance fund another, which SPEC-103 forbids. Add a test asserting the budget guard is venue-blind. (b) Betfair commission is charged on the NET MARKET result (SPEC-080), so a per-bet rate is only valid because v1 enforces one selection per market; the venue registry must assert that precondition. (c) Whether to open an account at a second exchange is a founder decision with a responsible-gambling dimension given the account history in ADR 0015/0020 — this platform records venues, it does not recommend opening accounts, and nothing here should read as encouragement to do so. (d) Placement stays manual and human; nothing in this change places, cancels or amends an order.

**Rule conflicts.** NONE. Back-only, pre-off, one selection per market, flat stake, single hard aggregate loss budget, manual placement — all unchanged. This adds provenance to the record and adds a refusal path; it removes no guard.

## 7. Verify the actual Betfair commission rate and stop hard-coding 2% — it is worth more ROI than the entire execution-cost haircut

**Status:** OPEN · **Area:** commission / break-even / settlement · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** hours

**Mechanism.** Commission is charged on gross winnings, so ROI moves almost one-for-one with the rate times gross winnings per unit staked. Settle: profit = (O-1)(1-c) on a win, -1 on a loss (`exchange_settlement.settle`). Therefore dROI/dc = -G where G = E[1{win}(O-1)] is mean gross winnings per unit stake. From TE-0019's supported reading (ROI = +2.63% at c = 0.02), 0.98*G - (1-W) = 0.0263, so G = (1.0263 - W)/0.98. For a plausible fired-bet win rate W in [0.35, 0.45] that is G in [0.588, 0.690]. A move from 2% to 5% therefore costs 0.03*G = 1.76 to 2.07 percentage points of ROI. Separately, a wrong c corrupts the FIRING rule itself: break_even = 1/(1+(O-1)(1-c)) rises by roughly 0.5 to 0.9 probability points going 2% -> 5%, so every bet whose predicted edge sits inside that strip is fired on a break-even bar that is too low and is actually negative EV at settlement. The error is invisible in a backtest that makes it consistently, which is exactly what has happened for eleven years of measurements.

**Expected magnitude.** If the true rate is 5% rather than 2%: -1.8 to -2.1 percentage points of ROI, i.e. TE-0019's +2.63% becomes roughly +0.6%, and TE-0014's +2.33% supported reading becomes roughly +0.4%. That single unverified parameter is LARGER than TE-0020's central execution-cost haircut (1.38pp) and comparable to the whole measured edge. If the rate is confirmed at 2%, the gain is zero but a load-bearing assumption becomes a fact and the money statement stops carrying an open caveat. Cost to find out: one login, and one statement line after the trial's first settled market. This is the highest expected-value-per-hour item in the audit.

**Evidence.** docs/research/findings/DR-TENNIS-MICROSTRUCTURE-001-findings.md (assessment section: 2% 'correct only conditionally', AU caveat, Expert Fee, Premium Charge ended 2025-01). docs/decisions/0020-account-restored-bounded-manual-trial.md (account restored 2026-07-29; statement is truth). tennis-edge/tennis_edge/upcoming.py:123-134 (break_even_probability). supabase/functions/tips/scoring.ts:31 (COMMISSION = 0.02). supabase/functions/tips/index.ts recordBet (`commission: 0.02` literal). tennis-edge/tennis_edge/experiments/exchange_settlement.py:118-121 (profit = gross*(1-rate)). SPEC-080 (commission on the net market result, once), SPEC-081 (statement is truth).

**How to verify.** Two independent reads that must agree: (1) the rate Betfair displays for the account's current package; (2) the commission actually deducted on the first settled market, read off the account statement. Then re-run `exchange_settlement.main()` with the verified rate and report the restated ROI and CI beside the 2% row — both rows, never the flattering one alone. Add a unit test that the break-even function and the TS port agree to the digit at the verified rate (the golden-vector harness already pins the pair), and a test that `recordBet` refuses when `commission_rate_source` is ASSUMPTION. Compute, from the existing `Bet.edge` field, the share of fired bets whose edge falls inside the break-even shift — that is the count of bets the wrong rate mis-fires.

**Risks.** The rate change is only defensible as a correction of a labelled assumption to a verified fact — it must be pre-declared and applied regardless of direction, because the likely direction makes every displayed edge WORSE. Re-costing the eleven-year backtest at a newly discovered rate is a restatement of prior findings, not a new result, and must be published as such (TE-0020's standing money statement would need a banded restatement). A per-account rate is not a universal fact, so the stored value must carry its source and its verification date and go stale. Risk of scope creep into 'optimise the rewards package' — out of scope; this is measurement, not a purchase decision.

**Rule conflicts.** NONE. This is not threshold-fitting: the parameter is a fact about the account, not a value chosen to improve a number, and the likely correction is adverse. No LLM prices anything. It does change which bets the display fires on, so it is a governed change to a money module (l5_decision-equivalent) and needs the tests-first, human-approved slice discipline.

## 8. Replace the ASSUMED execution-cost fraction with a DIRECTLY MEASURED LTP-to-crossable gap from the ADVANCED corpus already on disk

**Status:** OPEN · **Area:** execution cost / money-claim banding · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** days

**Mechanism.** The money verdict currently reads 'spans zero' because its pessimistic endpoint (f = 1.0, a full round-trip spread paid on one crossing) drives the interval to -0.09%. I measured the real distribution on a 900-market random sample of the corpus at T-600s (1,800 sides, outcome-free): the LTP sits at a mean of 0.353 of the way from best back to best lay, i.e. the true unconditional cost is roughly a THIRD of a spread, not a half and certainly not a whole one. On the population the model actually bets into — liquid, tight main-tour markets — the median gap is +0.72% of odds with a p90 of +1.72%, against a full spread of ~2.0%. So f = 1.0 is not a p90 bound; it is beyond p99. Replacing the assumed f with a measured distribution collapses a 2.7-percentage-point assumption band into a ~1.0-point measured band and moves its pessimistic end from below zero to clearly above it. Using TE-0020's own two costed points as the conversion (an extra half-Roll haircut of ~1.0-1.3% of odds costs 1.34pp of ROI, i.e. ~1.0-1.2 pp of ROI per 1pp of odds haircut), the measured cost translates as follows.

## 17. The decomposed serve layer has neither recency decay nor opponent adjustment — the package's own sibling module has both

## 18. Training weights every match from 2003 equally, against three independent measurements that the market changed in 2023/24

**Status:** OPEN · **Area:** model fitting (residual_model.fit_coefficients) · **Implementable now:** True · **Effort:** days

**Mechanism.** Ridge-logistic coefficients are an average of the relationship between features and market error across the training window. If that relationship is non-stationary — and three separate measurements say something about the anchor/outcome relationship moved — then equal weighting is a biased estimator of the current relationship, and the bias grows with the archive. The mechanism is the same one S8 measured from the other side: TE-0029 found a WEEK of state staleness costs +0.000879 nats, ~80% of the model's whole edge, dose-monotone. That is direct evidence this problem is strongly recency-dependent; there is no measurement of whether the COEFFICIENTS are too. TE-0032's untouched window is a faint corroborating hint (May +0.004632, June +0.002468, July +0.000201) but is explicitly recorded as a diagnostic at sample sizes too small to be a finding, and must not be treated as more.

**Expected magnitude.** UNKNOWN. There is no repo measurement of coefficient recency-sensitivity to anchor on. The bounding argument: the three regime diagnostics each individually span zero, so the effect they point at is at most layer-sized. Plausible range 0 to +0.0003 nats on a +0.001064 total, with a genuine chance of a null or a small loss (a shorter effective window discards data and raises coefficient variance, which at these effect sizes can dominate). This is the candidate most likely to return an honest zero.

**Evidence.** residual_model.py:163-201 (`fit_coefficients` — no weight term anywhere in the gradient/Hessian accumulation); TE-0019 Verdict 2 (bare-anchor gap +0.000781 -> +0.000430, deployment row spans zero once 2023–2026 enters); TE-0024 (gain by LTP-age band: +0.002139 / +0.000998 / +0.000349); TE-0028 (per-year delta sign flip at 2023/24, 'the third independent appearance of the same structural story'); TE-0029 (state-staleness dose-response, monotone, +0.000226 at lag 1 to +0.001032 at lag 7); TE-0031 ('the recurring post-2023 regime signal... is the one open scientific question').

**How to verify.** (i) Identity test: H -> infinity must reproduce the frozen artifact's coefficients bit-for-bit and the locked benchmark regression must stay byte-identical. (ii) Paired walk-forward, identical rows, day-clustered bootstrap, ONE confirmatory endpoint (weighted vs unweighted pooled gain over the anchor), family and bar declared before the run. (iii) Per-era decomposition published either way: pre-2023 and post-2023 gains separately, so a pooled pass driven entirely by one era is visible. (iv) Placebo: features detached from matches, judged on the placebo's CI (<= 0), matching the TE-0011 and S9 convention. (v) The fitted H series printed per fold; instability across folds is recorded as noise even if the pooled interval clears — the S9 sign-stability rule, applied here.

**Risks.** THE PRIMARY RISK IS THAT THIS BECOMES THRESHOLD FITTING WEARING A PRIOR'S CLOTHES — the exact failure `residual_model.py`'s own docstring warns about for L2 ('a penalty chosen by looking at the answer would be a fitted parameter wearing a prior's clothes'). Mitigation is structural, not procedural: H must be estimated by out-of-fold likelihood on training rows only, with the estimator's code committed before any scored run, and the fitted per-fold H values published whether or not the result clears. Second risk: shorter effective samples raise variance on the 22 correlated coefficients, and the existing L2=25 was chosen for the full-sample regime — it must NOT be re-chosen alongside H, or two parameters move at once and neither is attributable. Third: TE-0033 already measured that COMPOSITION is not what changed (composition CIs are an order of magnitude tighter than the within CIs and centred on zero), which sharpens the hypothesis but does not license the fix.

**Rule conflicts.** Must be registered as its own pre-registered slice with a declared expectation of a null. It does NOT conflict with the no-automatic-live-learning rule: H is fitted offline on historical rows in the same walk-forward as the coefficients, produces a new immutable versioned artifact, and requires human approval before serving — no live outcome updates any deployed parameter. It does NOT conflict with TE-0017 §8 item 17 (tuning against observed returns): the endpoint is out-of-sample log score, and H never sees a scored row.
